Spaghetti Recipe

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Marinara Sauce
  • Mushrooms
  • Garlic
  • Spinach
  1. Bring water to a boil.
  2. Add dry pasta to boiling water.
  3. Cook for 8-10 minutes.
  4. Remove pasta from heat.
  5. Drain pasta.
  6. Cook vegetables with oil in a pan.
  7. Add marinara sauce to cooked pasta.
  8. Add cooked vegetables to cooked pasta with sauce.
